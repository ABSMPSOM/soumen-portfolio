TeacherSphereX

Android-based teacher management and fee tracking system. Built to simplify student records, payments, and reporting directly from a mobile device.

Kotlin Android SQLite

Overview

TeacherSphereX is an Android-based teacher management and fee tracking application designed to simplify academic administration. It enables educators to efficiently manage student records, track payments, and monitor overall classroom data directly from a mobile device.With a clean and intuitive interface, the application streamlines daily teaching operations by reducing manual record-keeping and improving data organization. It provides a structured system for handling student information securely and efficiently, making it a practical tool for individual tutors and small educational setups.TeacherSphereX focuses on usability, speed, and reliability, ensuring that essential academic data is always accessible and easy to manage on the go.
